### 4. Bombay High Court Strikes IT Rules Amendment (Split Decision)
**Case:** *Kunal Kamra v. Union of India* | **Date:** Early Jan 2026 | **Priority:** HIGH

**What Happened:**
- Bombay HC held **Rule 3(1)(b)(v) of IT Intermediary Guidelines unconstitutional**
- Rule: Government could mandate fact-checking of information
- **Court's Finding:** Violates Article 14 (equality) & Article 21 (right to free expression)
- **Proportionality Test:** Failed—government cannot require platforms to enforce "truth"

**Key Quote from Judgment:**
> "Citizens have the right to free speech and expression, NOT the right to know truth. The State cannot mandate only 'true' information dissemination."

**Precedent:**
- Information in digital form NOT subject to Rule unless in print
- Fact-checking obligation on platforms = prior restraint (unconstitutional)
- Platform intermediary liability must be narrowly tailored

**Implication:** MeitY's IT Rules amendment (Oct 2025 draft) on AI-generated content verification faces similar constitutional challenges. Expect further litigation.
